### 📌 Overview & SEO Summary
Welcome to the ultimate curated directory of **AR remote assistance platforms**, **open-source remote guidance frameworks**, and **mixed reality collaboration tools**. Whether you are looking for enterprise-grade commercial solutions (such as *Salesforce Visual Remote Assistant*, *PTC Vuforia Chalk*, and *TeamViewer Frontline*), or self-hostable open-source alternatives (like *ClarifAI XR*, *Leap Brush*, and *ARENA*), this list covers category leaders, WebRTC streaming, and privacy-respecting remote support infrastructure.

**Key Market Context:**
- **Microsoft has announced that Dynamics 365 Remote Assist and Guides will end support on December 31, 2026**, and **PTC has made service termination and strategic adjustments for Vuforia Chalk** — signaling that some early mixed-reality remote assistance products are exiting the market or pivoting to ecosystem partnerships, while professional platforms and CRM/FSM-native integrated vendors will absorb the remaining demand .
- **ClarifAI XR** is a lightweight, real-time remote support platform designed for industrial environments, enabling technicians to stream camera views via mobile AR while remote experts overlay visual guidance (arrows, zones, annotations) aligned with real-world context using **Vue.js + Three.js + AR.js + MQTT + WebRTC** .
- **Leap Brush** from Magic Leap is an **AR Cloud reference application for Unity** that lets multiple ML2 devices draw in AR collaboratively, with a **spectator app** enabling remote participants to watch the action in real time .
- **ARENA** (ARENA-web-core) is a platform for **real-time mixed reality interaction between multiple users and the physical world**, built with **A-Frame and three.js**, supporting any WebXR-capable browser including HoloLens, Oculus Quest, and ARCore-enabled devices, with **Jitsi video conferencing in 3D environments** .
- **SurfaceCast** is an open-source framework for **collaborative mixed-presence tabletop interaction**, using **WebRTC with H.264/VP8, WebXR client support for Meta Quest and HoloLens 2, and GStreamer-based media processing** .
- **TeleopXR** transforms VR/AR headsets into **precise robot controllers** with **WebRTC video streaming, VR/Passthrough modes, and real-time IK control** — while primarily a teleoperation tool, its architecture is directly relevant to remote assistance scenarios requiring expert control of field equipment .
- **Remote Assistant (stonerey)** provides **AR remote assistance using HoloLens 2 and mobile** with **Unity and Mixed Reality Toolkit** .
- **AR-Remote-Support (AgoraIO)** is a **proof-of-concept remote support app similar to Vuforia Chalk**, built using **ARKit and Agora.io Video SDK** .

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects

*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[ClarifAI XR](https://github.com/virgiledjimgou/clarifai)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/virgiledjimgou/clarifai?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/virgiledjimgou/clarifai/stargazers)  
  **Lightweight, real-time remote support platform for industrial environments**, open-source. **Technicians stream camera view via mobile AR interface** while **remote experts overlay visual guidance (arrows, zones, annotations)** aligned with real-world context . **Vue.js + Three.js + AR.js + MQTT + WebRTC** . **Core modules**: ARClient (camera and marker tracking), AnnotationOverlay (arrows, highlights, text notes), ThreeScene (3D overlays), MqttSyncClient (commands from expert), WebRTCModule (live video stream) . **Expert Dashboard** with AnnotationPanel (draw and guide in real-time), MqttPublisher, and VideoFeedViewer. **Use cases**: remote machine diagnostics, expert-guided assembly/maintenance, AR-enabled support in resource-limited areas . **The most production-ready open-source AR remote assistance platform** . 🧠

- **[Leap Brush](https://github.com/magicleap/LeapBrush)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/magicleap/LeapBrush?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/magicleap/LeapBrush/stargazers)  
  **Magic Leap 2's AR Cloud reference application for collaborative AR drawing**, open-source. **Multiple ML2 devices can draw in AR together** — paintbrush with colors, poly tool with segmented dimmer, and 3D model placement . **Spectator App enables remote participation** — observers watch the action in real-time as participants create . **Multi-user and spectator capabilities via Leap Brush Server** . **Unity-based** with AR Cloud integration. **The most complete open-source collaborative AR drawing platform** . 🎨

- **[ARENA (ARENA-web-core)](https://github.com/EdwardLu2018/arena-web-core)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/EdwardLu2018/arena-web-core?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/EdwardLu2018/arena-web-core/stargazers)  
  **Platform for real-time mixed reality interaction between multiple users and the physical world**, open-source. **Built with A-Frame and three.js** — **works with any WebXR-capable browser** including **HoloLens, Oculus Quest, Vive, and ARCore-enabled devices** . **Publish/subscribe system for collaboration** . **Jitsi video conferencing in 3D environment** . **WASM for distributed XR applications** . **The most platform-agnostic open-source XR collaboration environment** . 🌐

- **[SurfaceCast](https://github.com/floe/surfacecast)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/floe/surfacecast?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/floe/surfacecast/stargazers)  
  **Collaborative mixed-presence tabletop interaction framework**, open-source. **WebRTC with H.264/VP8** for video streaming . **WebXR client verified on Meta Quest 1/2 and Microsoft HoloLens 2** using built-in browsers . **GStreamer-based media processing** . **Supports laptop, tablet, VR, projector, and interactive tabletop configurations** . **The most hardware-flexible open-source mixed-presence platform** . 🖥️

- **[TeleopXR](https://github.com/qrafty-ai/teleop_xr)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/qrafty-ai/teleop_xr?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/qrafty-ai/teleop_xr/stargazers)  
  **Transform VR/AR headset into a precise robot controller**, open-source. **WebRTC video streaming with ultra-low latency** . **VR/Passthrough modes** for immersive or AR situational awareness . **Real-time 3D robot visualization (digital twin)** . **Whole-Body IK for precise teleoperation** . **Supported robots**: Unitree H1, Franka Emika Panda, SO101, OpenArm . **Directly applicable to remote assistance scenarios requiring expert control of field equipment** . 🤖

- **[Remote Assistant (stonerey)](https://github.com/stonerey/RemoteAssistant)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/stonerey/RemoteAssistant?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/stonerey/RemoteAssistant/stargazers)  
  **Remote Assist for HoloLens 2 and mobile devices**, open-source. **AR remote assistance using HoloLens 2 and mobile** . **Unity-based** with Mixed Reality Toolkit. 🥽

- **[RemoteAssistantAR (icywind)](https://github.com/icywind/RemoteAssistantAR)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/icywind/RemoteAssistantAR?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/icywind/RemoteAssistantAR/stargazers)  
  **Realtime remote chat and assistant APP with AR support**, open-source. **Remote chat and assistant with AR support** . **Unity-based** . 💬

- **[AR-Remote-Support (AgoraIO)](https://github.com/AgoraIO-Community/AR-Remote-Support)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/AgoraIO-Community/AR-Remote-Support?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/AgoraIO-Community/AR-Remote-Support/stargazers)  
  **POC of remote support app similar to Vuforia Chalk**, open-source. **Built using ARKit and Agora.io's Video SDK** . **ARKit + Agora.io video SDK** . 📱

- **[Remote Assistant AR (Unity)](https://relatedrepos.com/gh/AgoraIO-Community/AR-Remote-Support)**  
  **Provide remote assistance using augmented reality**, open-source. **AR remote assistance reference implementation** . 🔧

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Remote Assist and Guides will end support on December 31, 2026** . **PTC has made service termination and strategic adjustments for Vuforia Chalk** . **Plan migrations to professional platforms or CRM/FSM-native integrated vendors**.
- **The industry is shifting from simple video+annotation to visual AI + knowledge accumulation + process automation** — early products emphasized remote experts seeing the site and circling problems; current leaders are integrating **session recording, automatic summaries, AI reports, visual recognition, ticket closure, and knowledge base accumulation** as core features .
- **Open-source AR remote assistance tools are not turnkey** — **ClarifAI XR requires Vue.js, Three.js, AR.js, MQTT, and WebRTC setup** . **Leap Brush requires Magic Leap 2 devices, Unity, and AR Cloud environment** . **ARENA requires Docker for self-hosting and WebXR-capable browsers** . **Always validate video latency, annotation alignment, and multi-user synchronization with a proof-of-concept** before production deployment. 🥽
